ENVS 1400 S02 FALL25- SUSTAINABLE DESIGN FOR THE BUILT ENVITONMENT

Spring 2025

Architecture

Project 2 explores domestic architecture through the lens of a single life process, as defined by Hannes Meyer’s Building manifesto. Developed in two phases and Sex Life as the process, the project begins with the design of a room dedicated to a chosen domestic activity and expands into a house for a single occupant devoted to that activity. With themes of duality, tension, and spatial collision, the project investigates how form, material, and movement can heighten bodily awareness and ritual within domestic space.
